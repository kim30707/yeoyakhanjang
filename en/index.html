From the first conversation to handover

We start by agreeing on what the site should do and who will operate it.

  1. Describe the work

    Show your current website or workflow and tell us what you want to improve.

  2. Agree on scope and schedule

    We settle the features, materials, cost, timing and how we will check the result.

  3. Build and review together

    We review the display on desktop and mobile, then test the agreed features such as booking or inquiries.

  4. Handover and operation

    You receive a visual guide and checklist for the tasks you will handle.

A visual guide for your day-to-day tasks

This example is based on a client guide covering reservations, payments and cancellations. Your handover guide shows the relevant menus and steps for your project.
