We start by agreeing on what the site should do and who will operate it.

  1. Describe the work

    Show your current website or workflow and tell us what you want to improve.

  2. Agree on scope and schedule

  3. Build and review together

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what clients usually ask before a project starts.

Who is Yeyak Hanjang?

Yeyak Hanjang is the web and software brand of Saem Studio (셈제작소), a registered business in Korea (representative 허진영, business registration no. 344-25-02293). We build websites, booking and ordering screens, internal tools and apps around how each business operates.

What can you build?

Business websites with lodging or performance booking, pre-order pickup and in-store QR ordering, internal tools and ERP, web and Android apps, online store and Smart Store setup, and food court order-number announcements.

Can you build booking websites for lodging or performances?

Yes. We built showtime and party-size selection with seat assignment in booking order for Dokkaebi Theatre, and a site that brings room photos and the booking inquiry together for AroundU guesthouse. Booking rules such as peak-season rates and cut-off dates follow your operation.

Does QR ordering connect to POS or online payment?

Pickup times, ready notices, cancellations, online payment and POS or announcement links are agreed after we review how your store operates. For order announcements, we check the tablet model, on-site network and amplifier connection before installation.

How are cost and schedule decided?

We first agree on the features, materials, review criteria and handover scope, then settle the cost and schedule. Website packages with prices and delivery times are listed on Fiverr and Upwork.

What does the process look like?

Describe the work, agree on scope and schedule, build and review together, then handover and operation. We review the display on desktop and mobile and test the agreed booking or inquiry flow together.

Do I need to share passwords or verification codes?

No. Keep passwords, verification codes and API keys out of chat and email. If account setup is difficult, we review the relevant screens together remotely at an agreed time, and you enter your own sign-ins and approvals.

Can I run the site myself after delivery?

Yes. You receive a visual guide and checklist for the tasks you handle, and operation stays in your own account.

How do I contact you?

Prepare an email with the inquiry form on this page, or start on Fiverr or Upwork. Korean clients can also use the Korean page and Kmong.
